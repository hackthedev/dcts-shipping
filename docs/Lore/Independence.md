# Independence

For the long-term success of DCTS, staying as independent as possible is essential.

Platforms often decay over time when investors, sponsors or external interests start shaping decisions. This has happened many times before, and DCTS is built to avoid that path.

Because of that, DCTS will never accept investors, sponsored servers or sponsorships that influence the project. DCTS is funded only through donations, keeping development focused on the community and the long-term health of the project.
